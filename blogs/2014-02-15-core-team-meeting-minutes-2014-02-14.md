Although most of our collaboration takes place on Github, IRC ( #emberjs on freenode.net), and our Discourse site the Ember.js Core Team meets privately every Friday at 2pm EST/11am PST through Google Hangout for a weekly discussion of all things Ember. If you have a topic you'd like to see covered, contact your favorite core team member and let them know! Attendees @ebryn, @krisselden, @machty, @stefanpenner, @tomdale, @trek, @wagenet, @wycats Topics ES6ifying core Last month we completed the migration of ember-data to the ES6 module syntax.
