We are thrilled to announce that we will be adding to the spring excitement surrounding Ember Octane and EmberConf 2019 with the release of the fifth annual Ember Community Survey, and as always, we want your input before we go live! This year we're making an effort to move much of the survey process to the ember-learn/annual-community-survey GitHub repo . Please review the the list of 2019 survey questions this before February 17th and submit feedback as an issues on that repo .
