Although most of our collaboration takes place on Github, IRC ( #emberjs on freenode.net), and our Discourse site the Ember.js Core Team meets privately every Friday at 2pm EST/11am PST through Google Hangout for a weekly discussion of all things Ember. If you have a topic you'd like to see covered, contact your favorite core team member and let them know! Attendees @krisselden , @machty , @mixonic @_mmun , @rwjblue , @trek , @stefanpenner , @wagenet , @wycats Face to Face July 2014 Last week the core team (and those on path to core) met in New York City for our periodic face to face meeting.
