Although most of our collaboration takes place on Github, IRC ( #emberjs on freenode.net), and our Discourse site the Ember.js Core Team meets privately every Friday at 2pm EST/11am PST through Google Hangout for a weekly discussion of all things Ember. If you have a topic you'd like to see covered, contact your favorite core team member and let them know!
